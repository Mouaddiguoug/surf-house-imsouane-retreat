## What guests keep saying

Across the 50 most recent Booking.com reviews and the Google reviews, the same
five things come up, in this order of frequency:

1. **Location** — "30 metres" from the bay, surf check with a coffee in hand,
   both spots visible from the rooftop, 1–5 minutes to the water.
2. **Abdellah and the staff** — named in a dozen reviews; kind, always
   available, returned a forgotten camera, cooked for a sick guest and called
   a doctor.
3. **Clean** — rooms and bathrooms swept and sanitised daily; "never stayed in
   a hostel that was so well organized and clean".
4. **Calm, family atmosphere** — "felt like home", chill rather than party,
   good for solo travellers, "everyone was in the same vibe".
5. **Rooftop, breakfast and the quiver** — yoga mats and weights on the roof,
   eggs and pancakes at 8–10, boards to rent "for the best price in town".

The recurring soft spots, for the record: a single toilet for the eight-bed
dorm, toilet paper running out, the wetsuits-outside rule (which some guests
praise and some resent), and "not the most social hostel".
